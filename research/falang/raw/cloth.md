### Verified source

Miyoko Kawahito, **Natural and Synthetic Indigo Color in Silk and Cotton Cloth**, *Sen'i Gakkaishi* 62(1), 1–5 (2006), DOI [10.2115/fiber.62.1](https://doi.org/10.2115/fiber.62.1). Opened [publisher article page](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/fiber/62/1/62_1_1/_article); PDF link: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/fiber/62/1/62_1_1/_pdf . [A]

The publisher abstract confirms spectrophotometric comparison of natural- and synthetic-indigo silk and natural-indigo cotton, analysed using CIELAB. It reports a brighter silk colour than cotton with natural indigo. [A] The full-text PDF is free access on the publisher page, but its attempted open timed out in this session; therefore no reflectance ordinate was transcribed or digitised. [I]
