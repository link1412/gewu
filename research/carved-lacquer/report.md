# 剔红 / 剔犀: evidence for a physically based material

Research date: 2026-10-29. **Partial delivery: research notes and numerical tables are saved; no original PDF, micrograph, or photograph was successfully downloaded.**

The strongest new result is a thickness-calibrated visible transmission study of cured clear urushi. Useful additional evidence includes microscope-observed lacquer stratigraphy, a measured local carving depth, museum descriptions of rounded V cuts, and identified photographs with catalog dimensions. Historical pigment loading, cinnabar grain dimensions in carved-lacquer sections, individual marker-band thicknesses, and actual diaper-cell pitches remain unresolved.

**Acquisition:** only indexed text and source links were retrieved; no original files or screenshots were saved. A link or an indexed caption is not counted here as a saved image. Consequently, image sharpness, maximum available resolution, wear details, and figure ordinates could not be visually verified.

## Evidence labels

- **M — measured / observed by source:** author-reported experiment or microscopic observation. We have not repeated the measurement.
- **C — museum catalog:** institution-reported dimensions or object description; measurement method generally unspecified.
- **R — recipe / practitioner report:** stated preparation or craft procedure, with its denominator and context retained.
- **F — read off a figure:** numerical digitization performed in this research. **None accepted**, because figure images could not be inspected.
- **S — secondary claim:** numerical statement not verified against its originating measurement.
- **I — inference:** our arithmetic or geometric interpretation, not a new measurement.

## 1. Cured clear lacquer: visible transmission with dry thickness

**S1.** Hiroshi Oyabu and Toru Asami, “Effects of Curing Condition on Transparency of Urushi Films,” *Journal of the Japan Society of Colour Material* **60**(2), 94–99 (1987), [DOI 10.4011/shikizai1937.60.94](https://doi.org/10.4011/shikizai1937.60.94). [Publisher page](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/shikizai1937/60/2/60_94/_article/-char/en); [free original PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/shikizai1937/60/2/60_94/_pdf/-char/en).

This is unpigmented **sugurome-urushi**, refined lacquer without added orpiment or oils, rather than urushiol dissolved in a solvent.

| Quantity | Value | Evidence and exact location |
|---|---|---|
| Actual dry detached-film thickness | **30 ± 3 µm** | **M**, S1 §2.2.3, printed p.95. |
| Published spectrum thickness | **30 µm** | **M**, authors' Lambert-law correction; §2.2.3 and §3.3. |
| Visible acquisition range / interval | **380–780 nm / 5 nm** | **M**, §2.2.3, Shimadzu UV-350A. |
| Initial cure | **24 h; 20 or 30 °C; 60, 70, 80, 90, or 98% RH** | **M**, experimental settings, §2.1.2. |
| Humidity comparison spectra | **Fig.5, p.97; 30 °C** | Primary measured transmission curves; not digitized here. |
| Time comparison spectra | **Fig.6, p.97; 20 °C / 80% RH; exposure through 90 days** | Primary measured curves; subsequent natural light from a north window. |

**What is available:** an identified original figure with the required film thickness. **What is missing locally:** the PDF/figure and wavelength–transmission ordinates. No artificial spectrum CSV has been created.

For PBR, `−ln(T)/d` would initially be **apparent attenuation**, not independently measured absorption: surface reflection and scattering must be accounted for. Choose the curing/exposure condition before digitizing. The material's origin and aging history also limit transfer to historical Chinese lacquer. See [spectrum_notes.md](spectrum_notes.md) for rejected near-matches involving liquid solutions.

**No lacquer-specific measured V-gouge included angle or round-gouge radius was verified.** Tools were individually ground, and a visible V cut need not have been produced by one fixed-profile V gouge. The Museum of Hubei's [Chang Bei transcript](https://wlt.hubei.gov.cn/hbmoa/ppzl/hbgjqysnz/xgwz/202007/t20200708_2572813.shtml) describes both trough and flat knives and shallow versus steep cuts; its historical profile distinctions are secondary claims, not angular measurements.

**Diaper pitch remains unmeasured.** No credible source found here supplies cell sizes in mm for identified 天锦 / 地锦 / 水锦 examples. The NGV conservator identifies land, water, and sky pattern families; Met 2020.323.1a-b is explicitly cataloged with all three zones. Neither establishes a universal pitch. Garner's “Diaper Backgrounds on Chinese Carved Lacquer,” *Ars Orientalis* 6 (1966),165–189, is an [unacquired object-based study](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4629227), not a verified measurement source here.

**Geometry inference:** distinguish normal layer thickness from exposed band width. For a planar cut at angle θ to the layer plane, surface-band width is `t/sin(θ)` and horizontal projected width is `t/tan(θ)`. A photographed band width is therefore not automatically a coat thickness. For cell pitch, use a full calibrated view and repeat-to-repeat pixel spans in the same surface plane; apply perspective/curvature correction. No pixel measurements were invented without a viewable image.

The helper does not resolve Smithsonian manifests or establish maximum image resolution, digitize spectra, or measure diaper cells. Those remain required follow-up work.
