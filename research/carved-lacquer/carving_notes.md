# Carving research notes

Research date: 2026-10-29. Scope: knife profiles, relief depths, and actual diaper-ground dimensions. Source classifications distinguish **measured by source**, **craft practitioner claim**, **secondary historical claim**, and **inference**. No generic woodworking tool specifications have been substituted for lacquer-carving evidence.

### Anhui Zhang Cheng tixi box: verified description versus unverified dimensions

An article published under **安徽博物院**'s source attribution, “云赏析\|‘张成造’剔犀云纹漆盒,” 2020-03-08, states **three red bands visible in the cuts**, **height 9.5 cm**, **diameter 14.5 cm**. These are **museum-attributed object description/catalog dimensions**, not a cross-sectional measurement: [museum article hosted on Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/378529529_744601). Other secondary sources give a different height, so the explicitly museum-attributed article is preferred for identification.

The often-repeated **1 cm knife depth and approximately 2 mm spacing between three red bands** could **not be verified from a primary source**. The closest located statement is “皖韵徽风，江淮要地——安徽博物院馆藏24件珍宝,” 2022-04-26, [secondary article](https://www.sohu.com/a/541017414_121119352), with the same figures also repeated by [Sina user post, 2025-07-09](https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5186477740265512.html). Classify both figures as **secondary claims only**, with measurement method and direction unspecified. In particular, do not use 2 mm as the normal thickness of a black band: it might describe spacing measured along the exposed sloping cut. The retired Anhui Museum original page was identified as <https://www.ahm.cn/cangpindetail_161_133%2C134.jsp> and its [2013 archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20130404040934/http%3A//www.ahm.cn/cangpindetail_161_133%2C134.jsp), but both failed to load. The museum-attributed 2020 article confirms three bands but supplies neither 1 cm nor 2 mm.

## Profile evidence useful to geometry

**A brocade-ground ridge should widen at its foot.** Palace Museum lacquer conservator Zhang Jun says that, when restoring geometric fret and diaper grounds, the knife should enter slightly outward rather than vertically; the raised lines are consequently trapezoidal, wider toward the ground, giving a larger attachment area. This is **firsthand conservation/craft instruction**, no numeric slope supplied. This is stronger evidence for a tapered ridge profile than imposing an arbitrary stock V-gouge angle. Citation: 张军, “雕漆纹饰的修复,” *紫禁城* 2020, no. 7, pp. 150–153, especially p. 152, [original PDF](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2020/07/28/u5f1ff3ca123c0.pdf).

**Tools are individually ground, and surface finishing erases tool marks.** Palace Museum article describes separate knives for incision, shaving the ground plane, hooked detail lines, and scraping edges/tool marks; artisans grind their own tools to practical requirements. It also explains that blunt tools compress the soft lacquer and can wrinkle or chip adjacent layers. **Firsthand conservation/craft explanation**, no included angles or round-gouge radii. Citation: *紫禁城* 2019, no. 8, pp. 142–144 visible in the indexed PDF; full title/author could not be verified because opening the source timed out. [Original PDF](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2019/10/24/u5db120fed00dd.pdf). Do not assign a guessed bibliographic title to this source.

**Different tixi sections are intentional.** Chang Bei's invited paper for the 2016 Hubei International Triennial of Lacquer Art discusses the older southern profile as rounded/tile-like and the northern profile as steep/deep, attributing this distinction to *Xiushi lu*. It describes contemporary maker Chen Qinli using both trough and flat knives: near-vertical cuts crowd the exposed bands together, shallowly shaving cuts broaden them. **Secondary historical claim plus direct commentary on the maker's work**, no angle or radius supplied. [Hubei Museum of Art, official transcript](https://wlt.hubei.gov.cn/hbmoa/ppzl/hbgjqysnz/xgwz/202007/t20200708_2572813.shtml).

**One documented object combines V grooves and rounded troughs.** The Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art describes F1995.2a-b as having deep V-shaped grooves with a rounded trough (and nine alternating color bands). This is **museum object description**, not a measured tool cross-section. Its catalog dimensions are 5.2 × 7.7 × 7.7 cm. [Object record](https://asia-archive.si.edu/object/F1995.2a-b/). Parent research owns the image-download record.

## Diaper grounds and dimensions: unresolved

No trustworthy source located in this search supplied **actual cell pitch in mm** for 天锦 / 地锦 / 水锦 on an identified real object. No figure-derived numerical pitch has been asserted. Nor was an included V-gouge angle or round-gouge radius found in a lacquer-specific primary source.

The official draft vocational standard *漆器制作工 国家职业标准（征求意见稿）*, made available in March 2024, specifically treats the ability to produce/inspect 天锦、地锦、水锦 as a skill, but gives no numerical pattern pitch. [PDF](https://www.osta.org.cn/zcwj/attachment/20240325_50.pdf), table entry 3.5.2 on printed p. 16. This does not establish a universal standard size.

Harry Garner's object-based article remains an appropriate lead for pattern identification and history: “Diaper Backgrounds on Chinese Carved Lacquer,” *Ars Orientalis* **6** (1966), pp. 165–189, [JSTOR record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4629227). The full article was not acquired; do not attribute measurements to it from its title alone.

A particularly useful future calibrated image is LACMA **M.71.100.10a-b**, which visibly includes the three ground zones and worn upper relief. LACMA lists **height 9.7 cm, width 5.4 cm** (**museum catalog dimensions**): [object page](https://collections.lacma.org/object/29634). Full image URL exposed by image search: <https://collections-images.lacma.org/images/29634/29634-1-primary.webp>. Commons derivative: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/Snuff_Bottle_%28Biyanhu%29_with_Children_at_Play_LACMA_M.71.100.10a-b_%281_of_2%29_%28cropped%29.jpg>. That cropped derivative is 875 × 1036 pixels according to the [Commons category listing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category%3ACarved_lacquer_from_China); the crop must not be treated as the complete 54 mm bottle width.

For a defensible image estimate: use a full image in which the dimensioned silhouette and desired ground are both visible; measure the known object span in pixels, then measure several cell repeats near the central frontal area in the same plane. Compute pitch = known span × (repeat span / count) / full-object pixel span. Report both pixel measurements and a projection uncertainty. A curved snuff bottle needs local surface correction or a visibly frontal central patch. No numerical estimate should be made before viewing the downloaded image.
