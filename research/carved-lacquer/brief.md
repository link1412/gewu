# 剔红 / 剔犀 (Chinese carved lacquer): data for a physically based material

We are rendering carved red lacquer (剔红) and carved black-and-red lacquer (剔犀) as a physically based material, checked against real photographs. Already found (no need to repeat): urushi refractive index 1.52/1.55 (Tobunken 保存科学 45, 2006); 60° gloss 89–91 GU and Ra 0.13 µm of fresh Chinese lacquer films (Polymers 2025, 17, 1481); NGV fragment ~1 mm, 17 red layers; Met/Cleveland object photos 2011.120.1, 13.100.141, 1918.933.

Please find, and save the files into this folder:

1. **Visible absorption or transmission spectrum of a cured clear lacquer (透明漆 / 推光漆 / suki-urushi / urushiol) film** with its thickness, as numbers or a figure we can read.
2. **Red lacquer composition**: cinnabar/vermilion content in red lacquer (weight or volume fraction, traditional recipes 朱砂与漆的比例), cinnabar particle sizes in cross sections of carved lacquer; layer thicknesses and counts from cross sections of 剔红 and 剔犀 (thickness of the red and black bands, marker layers such as the thin black or yellow lines). Cross-section micrographs are welcome.
3. **Carving**: knife profiles (V-gouge angles, round gouges), relief depths, and the geometric diaper grounds 锦地 (天锦 / 地锦 / 水锦): their cell sizes in mm on real pieces.
4. **Photographs**, any licence (reference only), as large as available, where the scale can be worked out (a ruler, or a known object size): close-ups of 剔红 diaper grounds, of 剔犀 cuts showing the bands, of knife edges and worn high points. Download the image files and note each one's page URL and object size.

Deliver `report.md` in this folder: each number with its source (citation + URL) and whether it is measured, read off a figure, or a secondary claim; and the list of saved files.
