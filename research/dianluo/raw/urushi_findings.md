## Full-text primary source: actual polished black urushi

**Wonjoung Lee (李沅貞), Kosuke Mochizuki (望月宏祐), Hiroshi Oyabu (大藪泰).** “Relationship between Sensitivity Evaluation and Light Reflection Characteristics of Urushi (Asian Lacquer) Coatings with Black Roiro (Polished Black Color) Finish” / 黒呂色仕上げによる漆塗膜の感性評価と光反射特性の関係. *Transactions of Japan Society of Kansei Engineering* **22**(4), 353–360 (2023). DOI: [10.5057/jjske.TJSKE-D-23-00028](https://doi.org/10.5057/jjske.TJSKE-D-23-00028). [Landing page](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjske/22/4/22_TJSKE-D-23-00028/_article/-char/en); [full PDF, opened](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjske/22/4/22_TJSKE-D-23-00028/_pdf/-char/en).

[M] No1: black urushi, three applications, followed by three 上摺り applications with Japanese raw urushi; No4: black urushi, 胴摺り only. Fig.2 gives 20° gloss **99.77 versus 61.47 GU**. Figs.3–4 give SCI/SCE L* **25.78/2.05 versus 25.53/5.93**. Measurement: Rhopoint IQ-S, area 6×6.4 mm; CM-700d, diffuse illumination/8° reception, 400–700 nm (§§2.1, 2.3–2.4).

[M] Fig.5, p.358, plots reflected camera intensity versus estimated surface-normal angle on curved tea caddies, with illumination 50° from camera axis (§3.6). [I] These are useful profile references, not calibrated BRDF data: §2.5 explicitly says pixel values are device-dependent relative quantities without physical units. [I] Numeric reflectance spectra and roughness are **not found** here. [D] Gloss values are numeric labels printed in Fig.2, rather than estimates from bar height; retained [M] above because they are explicit source measurements.

## Independent black-urushi measurements

**Yoshikazu Aoki (青木善一), Kosuke Iai (居相浩介).** ステンレスの電解酸化発色と各種表面加工による製品の品質管理 / “Combination Development of Electrolytic Coloring and Various Surface Finishes of Stainless Steel.” *表面技術 / Journal of the Surface Finishing Society of Japan* **70**(12), 608–612 (2019). [Full PDF, opened](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/sfj/70/12/70_608/_pdf/-char/en).

[M] Black-urushi comparator sample ⑰: Table4 (p.611) gloss at 20°/60°/85° = **96.8/100.0/99.4**, measured using Konica Minolta GM-268A. Table3 gives SCI L*a*b*=**27.57,−0.02,−0.77**, SCE=**3.11,−0.06,−0.05**. [I] This is actual black urushi but not established as a historically specified 推光 formulation; coating recipe and finishing history **not found**. [I] Table1 roughness values concern steel specimens; do **not** assign them to urushi. [I] Gloss units are not percent total reflectance.

## Refractive-index evidence: important distinctions

**Yukiyo Fujishima, Noritaka Kajii, Toshiro Egashira.** 漆液の簡易品質評価に関する研究－簡易屈折計による評価－ / “Study of Simple Quality Evaluation of Urushi Liquid—Evaluation by a Simple Refractometer.” *石川県工業試験場研究報告* **63**, 33–36 (2014). [Full institutional PDF, opened](https://www.irii.jp/randd/theme/h25/pdf/study009.pdf).

[M] §3.1 (p.35): **raw liquid sap nD=1.495–1.530**, measurements at **20±2°C**. Refractometer upper limit **1.5318**; refined liquid urushi exceeded that limit. [I] Neither result is a measurement of a cured urushi film. [I] Cured-film refractive index and 400–700 nm dispersion remain **not found**.

**Sho Yoshida, Asumi Sano, Yutaro Shimode, Chieko Narita; Oike & Co.** 漆黒調積層体、及びその製造方法. Patent **JP2016168772A**, published 23 September 2016. [Full patent, opened](https://patents.google.com/patent/JP2016168772A/ja).

[M] Experiments 1–2 use synthetic acrylic-urethane/fluorene-acrylic coatings, not urushi. [D] Their **n=1.55–1.56** synthetic finish best matches black-roiro urushi appearance; synthetic **4.5–5.0%** reflectance and **94–105** 60° gloss are described as close to urushi (Experiment 3). [I] These are surrogate properties and **must not be relabelled as measured cured-urushi n**. [D] Figs.3a and 4a contain an actual urushi comparator spectrum, potentially digitizable; wavelength range, geometry, and plotted values were not independently recovered, so no numerical spectrum is delivered.
