## Modern Yangzhou factory technical specification

**Primary documentary source:** 嵇俊、吴志勇、吴涛、陈静、周璇 (principal drafters), 扬州漆器厂有限责任公司 (drafting organisation), *点螺漆器技术规范 / Technical specification for point screw lacquer*, Jiangsu local standard **DB32/T 4532—2023**, issued 2023-07-25, effective 2023-08-25. Opened full seven-page PDF: https://qxb-img-osscache.qixin.com/standards/d3133316e20b3a39849380ee5ff31c72.pdf . Cited pages: printed pp. 1–3 (PDF pp. 4–6). [D]

- The standard lists 珍珠贝, 鲍鱼贝 (also called “盘大鲍”, 石决明), 夜光螺, and 金耳朵 (river-mussel shell lip). It provides common names, **not biological species identifications**. [D]
- Remove outer and calcareous layers, select attractive, tough inner nacre, mechanically thin, then wet grind using sandpaper/oilstone to **0.03–0.10 mm**. This source describes grinding; boiling and splitting: **not found**. [D]
- The work sequence selects natural colour, draws patterns on suitable-colour shell, cuts dots/lines/areas, transfers with a needle point, and incises details. It gives **no lateral size distributions**. [D]
- Its definition includes grinding the inlay level with lacquer; its finishing step also applies lacquer over the object, dries, grinds and polishes, with final 磨显 quality requirements. [D]
- **Rendering consequence:** both lacquer application and exposure by grinding occur in the documented process. It is unjustified to equate the word 罩漆 with a specified surviving transparent layer, or to assert universal bare nacre. Residual film after polishing, clear/black identity at each pass, and Qing object cross sections: **not found**. [I]
- Black-pigment/iron recipe, backing colour, final nanoscale roughness: **not specified**. [D]

## Classical colour selection and coloured backing — verified transcription, edition unresolved

**Opened transcription:** 黄成撰、杨明注, *髹饰录*, 识典古籍, book identifier CADAL02095380, 坤集、填嵌第七, https://www.shidianguji.com/book/CADAL02095380/chapter/1lb3wb3bo4t2r . A second opened page, https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/book/CADAL02095380/chapter/1lb3wb1pl9s7e , includes 朱启钤's 弁言 dated **“民國十六年丁卯二月，紫江朱啓鈐識。”** and his description of restoring the text and printing it. [D] This establishes a transcription containing the **1927 丁卯 recension's preface**; printer and physical source-copy metadata are **not found**, so direct scan-level edition fidelity is unverified. [I]

Exact passage shown on that page (punctuation retained as displayed; source transcribes 简体):

Exact backing passage displayed (its apparent OCR/transcription issue “琴微” is **not corrected silently**):

> 衬色蜔嵌，即色底螺钿也。其文宜花鸟草虫，各色莹彻焕然如佛郎嵌。又加金银衬者，俨似嵌金银片子琴微用之亦好矣。

The transcription attests intentional colour selection and coloured / gold-and-silver backing as named techniques; it does **not** show that all 点螺 has backing, nor that a particular Qing round box has it. [D]/[I]

## Boiling/splitting versus grinding — corroborating conservation report

**Opened 10-page report extract:** *傳統螺鈿漆器工藝技法與保存維護*, hosted by Taiwan's National Culture and Arts Foundation project archive, https://archive.ncafroc.org.tw/upload/result/1854-A1005/1854-A1005_%E5%85%A7%E5%AE%B9%E6%91%98%E9%8C%84_%E8%AA%BF%E6%9F%A5%E7%A0%94%E7%A9%B6%E5%A0%B1%E5%91%8A_0_1560405012127.pdf , printed p. 2 (PDF p. 2), lines 38–42 of extracted text. Author and report date: **not found** in the opened extract. [D]

This is a substantive 10-page extract, **not merely an abstract** despite the filename 內容摘錄. It says thin shell may be obtained by **boiling, repeated hammering and layer stripping, then grinding**, **or** by cutting raw shell and grinding it paper-thin; subsequent steps are sticking, lacquer coating, grinding and polishing. It gives **no boiling conditions, species-specific split yields, final film thickness or dot/thread dimensions**. [D]

Thus both routes are documented for the broad thin-inlay tradition; the modern Yangzhou factory standard specifically prescribes mechanical grinding. Treating boiling/splitting as universal for Qing Yangzhou 点螺 would exceed this evidence. [I]

## 江千里 and the saying

**Opened museum catalogue:** 上海博物馆, *清 江千里款黑漆嵌螺钿人物圆盒*, https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/m/article/id/CI00005056 . The museum calls 江千里 a late-Ming mother-of-pearl inlay craftsman, 字秋水, 浙江嘉兴人; the object's place of production is catalogued as 扬州. These distinguish reported birthplace from workshop association. [D]

The museum's exact saying is:

> 杯盘处处江秋水，卷轴家家査士标。

This verifies the **江秋水** form, not the brief's tentative “江千里” wording. The museum uses 查士标's name, whereas another search result used his 字二瞻; the latter has not been verified against an opened historical edition here. [D]/[I]

The box is Qing, wood, diameter 7.7 cm, height 2.7 cm; large shell pieces make rocks/figures, smaller pieces trees/railings, shell fragments the courtyard ground. [M] dimensions; [D] material and decoration. Object inventory number, separate lid height, and open licence: **not found** on this page. [I]
