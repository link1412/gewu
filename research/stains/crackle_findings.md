# Crackle dimensions: Q3–Q4

## Q3: Cell sizes

[D] Sophia Lahlil, Weidong Li and Ji Ming Xu, “Crack patterns morphology of ancient Chinese wares,” *The Old Potter’s Almanack* 18(1), 2013, pp. 1–9. DOI [10.11588/opa.2013.1.11773](https://doi.org/10.11588/opa.2013.1.11773). [Original PDF](https://journals.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/index.php/opa/article/download/11773/5630); [alternate PDF](https://www.dstretch.com/Lahlil%20et%20al.%20OPA%2018.1.pdf).

[D] Page 5 reports characteristic cell spacing **0.17–1.41 cm = 1.7–14.1 mm**, defined as **l = √(A/N)**, where A is the analysed area and N the domain count. Page 3 describes 23 Song/Yuan excavated sherds from Jiaotanxia, Laohudong and Fenghuang hill; inner/outer surfaces give 46 glazes. These are Guan and imitation Guan, not a securely attributed Ge-only dataset. Their ImageJ/DStretch method measures domains after crack enhancement. Thickness–spacing relationship is proposed for later investigation; paired archaeological glaze-thickness/cell-size data: **not found**.

[I] This spacing is not a diameter, an individual cell-size range, or separate gold-thread/iron-wire cell sizes. Use it only as an aggregate archaeological scale reference.

[D] Access qualification: alternate PDF opened as nine pages, but no text or usable image was returned. Above numbers are from indexed page excerpts, not an independently inspected PDF. Original Heidelberg route denied access.
