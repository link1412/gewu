### Mapping to pbrt-v4 and acceptance checks

- [I] Simulate water and pigment outside pbrt, export time-indexed concentration/deposition maps, and retain original dimensions/time units only when calibration establishes them. First test matched droplets/strokes and drying curves; simulator time steps alone do not establish physical seconds.
- [I] For a practical first render, map calibrated spectral reflection/transmission into **diffusetransmission** on a **bilinearmesh/trianglemesh** sheet, adding fibre-scale thickness/normal variation. This supports the travelling backlight but approximates lateral transport and incidence-angle behaviour.
- [I] For hero grazing views and edge spread, construct a finite-thickness paper slab with **interface** boundaries and a **homogeneous** scattering medium; animate a **diffuse area** light behind it for shot 38. Use transport parameters fitted to actual paper R/T, not unconverted K–M numbers. Mass-specific coefficients (m²/kg) require material density (kg/m³) to become path-length coefficients (m⁻¹); K–M two-flux scattering and full radiative-transfer scattering are not interchangeable without a mapping/fitting step.
- [I] A spatial ink absorption layer can use **uniformgrid/nanovdb** density; modelling absorbed ink and paper as independently varying coefficients within the same volume requires checking the chosen grid's parameterization or extending it. **rgbgrid** is an RGB-field route, so it cannot be assumed to preserve arbitrary measured spectra per voxel. Thin deposited ink plus a separate paper slab is a controllable approximation; ink inside fibres remains a gap.
- [I] Completion criteria for the film test: compare front/back spectral R/T and a back-lit ink edge at normal and grazing angles, then judge the moving-light sequence after the specified sensor/noise and **1080p, 5.1 Mb/s H.264** pipeline. No renders or codec tests were run in this research task; agreement to the reference film is unverified.

### pbrt-v4 mapping and practical gap

- **[D]** [Official pbrt-v4 material reference](https://pbrt.org/fileformat-v4#materials): `coateddiffuse`/`coatedconductor` are a dielectric interface over a diffuse/conductor base, with intervening-medium `albedo`, `g` and `thickness`; `conductor` accepts spectral eta/k, including named Au/Cu spectra. **[I]** For opaque Chinese enamel, `coateddiffuse` captures coloured diffuse return plus polished Fresnel highlights. For translucent enamel visibly revealing copper, `coatedconductor` is the nearer built-in two-layer approximation. Neither is an arbitrary stack with resolved bubbles. **[I]** Use triangle/PLY meshes for wires and polished-open pits; normal maps for unresolved pits; use a closed `dielectric` enamel shell with `homogeneous` medium and separate copper substrate for explicit thickness/embedded bubbles. This requires measured/calibrated extinction and scattering, not chemical concentrations alone.
- **[I] Biggest gap:** no confirmed reusable measured Chinese cloisonné visible BRDF/R/T or n/k/σa/σs, no representative enamel-layer thickness or bubble-diameter distribution; the measured **0.9 mm pore depth is only one defect**. Acquire colour-specific reflectance/transmission and microscopy of a matching sample before claiming physical spectral fidelity.
