# What we need

The photo's dark red front looks too dark and shows almost no glaze reflection compared with physics, while the sides are 3.5x brighter. We suspect either (a) the JPEG's tone curve crushes the shadows, (b) a polarizing filter / cross-polarisation in the Met's ceramics photography, or (c) black cards/flags. Please find:
