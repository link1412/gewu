# Ink on xuan paper: published physical and optical data

Research date: 2 November 2026. Scope: Chinese ink spreading in paper and observation with illumination behind the sheet.

**Reading the tables.** Values are tied to the named specimen and test conditions. “Measured” includes instrument measurements; “source-derived” means the authors infer a value from measurements; “assumed/model” or “prescribed” means a chosen simulation parameter or recipe. Review recommendations are labelled as syntheses. “Not found” means no verified numerical value was located in the inspected sources; it does not prove that none has ever been published. Exact unit conversions or arithmetic are labelled separately. No graph coordinates, pore radii, halo sizes, or missing material constants have been estimated here.

The source records give authors, year, title, journal and URL; table locators give the page, table, figure or equation supporting each entry. Printed page numbers take precedence over PDF viewer page numbers. Raw/unprocessed xuan, processed/sized xuan, laboratory nanocomposites, Japanese washi and other fallback papers are kept distinct.

### Notes and equations

**MoXi:** equations are in lattice units. Its modified equilibrium is

$$
f_i^{eq}=w_i\left\{\rho+\rho_0\psi\left[\frac{3}{c^2}\mathbf e_i\cdot\mathbf u+\frac{9}{2c^4}(\mathbf e_i\cdot\mathbf u)^2-\frac{3}{2c^2}\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf u\right]\right\},\qquad
\psi=\operatorname{smoothstep}(0,\alpha,\rho).
$$

Collision/streaming: $f_i(\mathbf x+\mathbf e_i\Delta t,t+\Delta t)=(1-\omega)f_i(\mathbf x,t)+\omega f_i^{eq}(\mathbf x,t)$. The viscosity relation is $\nu=(1/\omega-1/2)/3$. Partial bounce-back models permeability:

$$
f_i(\mathbf x,t+1)=\kappa_i(\mathbf x)f_k(\mathbf x,t)+[1-\kappa_i(\mathbf x)]f_i(\mathbf x-\mathbf e_i,t),\quad
\kappa=k_1+k_2G+k_3A+k_4g+k_5h,
$$

Here k is the opposite direction; κᵢ averages linked-site blocking factors. G, A, g and h represent grain, alum, glue and fixed pigment. Deposition uses $\max(1-\rho/\lambda,m)$ and seepage $\phi=\operatorname{clamp}(s,0,\pi-\rho)$. These are selected updates; remaining pigment-transfer rules are in §5.4. [Chu & Tai 2005, Eqs. (1), (5)–(8), §§5.2–5.4](https://cse.hkust.edu.hk/visgraph/MoXi/moxi.pdf).

**Curtis et al.:** pigment exchange before clamping is $\delta_\downarrow=g^k(1-h\gamma^k)\rho^k$, $\delta_\uparrow=d^k[1+(h-1)\gamma^k]\rho^k/\omega^k$; update $d^k\leftarrow d^k+\delta_\downarrow-\delta_\uparrow$, $g^k\leftarrow g^k+\delta_\uparrow-\delta_\downarrow$. Capillary capacity is $c=h(c_{max}-c_{min})+c_{min}$. Four-neighbour capillary transfer is $\Delta s=\max[0,\min(s_i-s_j,c_j-s_j)/4]$, gated by source/recipient saturation thresholds. Edge darkening reduces pressure by $p\leftarrow p-\eta(1-M')M$, with M′ the blurred wet mask. For KM rendering, $a=1+K/S$, $b=\sqrt{a^2-1}$, $C=a\sinh(bSx)+b\cosh(bSx)$, $R=\sinh(bSx)/C$, $T=b/C$; two layers combine as $R=R_1+T_1^2R_2/(1-R_1R_2)$, $T=T_1T_2/(1-R_1R_2)$. Their x is simulated pigment amount, not measured film thickness. [Curtis et al. 1997, pp. 424–426, §§4.1, 4.3.3, 4.5–4.6, 5.2](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/curtis-1997-cgw.pdf).

**Lee (2001):** the front-based algorithm decrements model liquid quantity $W(P')=W(P)-1$, uses normalized crossing count $A(P')=C(P')/C_{average}$, and deposited intensity $D(P')A(P')W(P')$. Initial-boundary filtering caps the next intensity by $\min[(1-g)I(P),D(P')A(P')W(P')]$. This is a prescribed rendering/front model, not a measured Lucas–Washburn coefficient. [Lee 2001, p. 304, Algorithm 2](https://www.scribd.com/document/350324646/Lee-2001).

**Huang et al. (2003):** $\operatorname{Absorbency}(p)=\mathrm{Base}+\mathrm{Var}\times\operatorname{rand}()$. Carbon is retained if $\mathrm{CarbonDiameter}>\mathrm{HoleDiameter}(p)$, otherwise follows water outflow. No measured distributions of either diameter are supplied by this rule. [PDF p. 3, §2.1 Eq. (1), §2.3 pseudocode](https://dspace.zcu.cz/bitstream/11025/1615/1/H89.pdf).

**Wang & Rokne (2007):** $Q=KJS$, or on a network edge $Q_m=K_mS_m(p_m-p_{m+1})/L_m$; interior nodes satisfy $\sum_iQ_i=0$, solved as a linear pressure system. Source pressure is set to zero and moving-front pressure to a prescribed p₀. The paper labels Q in m³/s, S in m², K in m/s, but calls J a pressure gradient; this is dimensionally consistent with those K units only for hydraulic-head gradient, not pressure in Pa/m. No correction or numerical physical calibration is inferred here. [pp. 273–275, Eqs. (2)–(14)](https://www.scitepress.org/PublishedPapers/2007/20719/20719.pdf).

**Deep-bed filtration:** a directly verified later experimental source writes the Iwasaki removal equation as $\partial C/\partial x=-\lambda C$ and uses $\lambda/\lambda_0=1-k\sigma$ under unfavourable surface conditions. Here λ has inverse-length units, σ is deposited amount, and k depends on its definition. Its measured collector system is latex/glass beads; a λ value for Chinese ink on xuan remains **not found**. [Jegatheesan & Vigneswaran 2000, “Accumulation of particle deposition” and “Experimental” section snippets](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0043135499003814). A fixed per-timestep capture fraction from a graphics paper is not automatically an Iwasaki inverse-length coefficient.

**Later measured adsorption/front model (nylon fallback):** Nicasy et al. use $C_b=C_{b,m}\kappa C_0/(1+\kappa C_0)$ and $S(t)=l(t)(1+\kappa C_0)/[1+\kappa(C_0+C_{b,m})]$. S and l are particle and liquid front depths in metres, C₀ is initial particle number concentration in m⁻³, and the final-depth fit uses l = 170 µm, the reported membrane thickness. [Nicasy et al. 2024, printed pp. 7–8, Eq. (6), Figs. 6–7](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/317661286/1-s2.0-S0927775724000074-main.pdf). The empirical fit is specific to latex/water/glycerol in nylon; it supplies no xuan/soot binding constant.
