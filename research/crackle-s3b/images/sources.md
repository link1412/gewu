## Scale convention

A printed microscope scale bar is local calibration: px/mm = native pixels along the bar / bar length in mm. Whole-object catalogue dimensions provide only a nominal projected scale; curved surfaces, oblique views, cropping and resizing prevent treating that number as local metrology. Pixel dimensions, bar lengths in pixels and the requested ≥20 px/mm threshold must be checked on downloaded originals. None of the additional candidates below is falsely certified to meet that threshold.

- Institution: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York.
- Object: *Sherd*, China, Song dynasty (960–1279), stoneware; Guan ware; accession **42.18.3**, gift of Judge Edgar Bromberger (1942).
- Catalogue: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/48503
- Image link exposed by that catalogue: https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/api/collection/v1/iiif/48503/1797498/main-image
- Object metadata endpoint (not successfully retrieved): https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/48503
- Museum dimensions: **9.2 × 4.8 cm** (catalogue measurements, independently verified in the museum record).
- Rights: museum page explicitly identifies this image as **Public Domain** and its Open Access images as unrestricted commercial and noncommercial use.
- Image pixel dimensions: not independently obtained here. **32 px/mm is supplied in the brief, not independently calibrated here.** The brief's 130 µm band width and +17% brightness also remain user-supplied image observations, not museum measurements. A scale based on the 92 mm sherd length would require measuring the object's projected pixel extent, rather than dividing total canvas height by 92 mm.
- Use: the correct photographic baseline for pale / light crackle bands. Download pending; intended local file `met_48503_42.18.3_original.jpg` (not present).
