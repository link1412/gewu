# Research brief: the representative Chinese reds as physically based materials

We render real materials from physics (spectral optics, measured parameters) and validate each against real photographs at a known scale. We are planning which red materials to build. For each red below, find:
- (1) the colourant and the physics of its colour, with measured spectra where available (reflectance or absorption, 380-780 nm);
- (2) two or three iconic objects that carry it: museum pieces with open-access images and physical sizes;
- (3) what the surface looks like in a close-up (texture, gloss, ageing).

## Rules (binding)

- Do not invent citations. Every fact needs a source: author, year, title, journal, and a DOI or URL. Tag each number: [M] measured in that source, [D] derived or read off a figure, [?] uncertain. If you cannot find something, write "not found".
- You cannot download files. List URLs instead.
- Budget: about 40 minutes.

## The reds
1. **朱砂 / 银朱** (cinnabar, vermilion; HgS).
   - Band gap; reflectance spectra of the pigment; the effect of particle size; darkening.
   - Where it is used:
     - 朱漆 (vermilion lacquer);
     - 剔红 (carved cinnabar lacquer): number of layers, layer thickness, how it is carved, how its surface looks;
     - 印泥 (seal paste); 朱砂墨; 朱批.
2. **铜红** (copper-red glazes: 郎窑红, 祭红 or 霁红, 釉里红, 钧红).
   - Copper nanoparticles: size, concentration, depth; their plasmon absorption; Cu2O.
   - Why the rim turns white (脱口, 灯草边).
   - Jun's red-purple splashes (copper red over opalescent blue).
   - Measured spectra.
3. **金红** (colloidal gold: 胭脂红 famille-rose enamel; Qing palace gold-ruby glass).
   - Particle size and plasmon peak; Purple of Cassius.
   - Faraday 1857 (Phil. Trans. 147), which also showed gold leaf transmitting green.
   - Measured spectra.
4. **铁红** (iron-oxide red).
   - Where it is used: 矾红 overglaze red; the red walls of the Forbidden City (which pigment, exactly? sources); red earthenware.
   - Hematite particle size and colour; measured spectra.
5. **铅丹** (red lead, Pb3O4).
   - 万年红纸: Qing book fly-leaves coated with red lead against insects. Sources.
   - Band gap; spectra.
6. **Organic reds for silk and paper.**
   - 红花 (carthamin), 茜草 (madder: alizarin, purpurin), 苏木 (sappanwood: brazilin), 紫胶 or 胭脂虫 (lac, cochineal).
   - Absorption spectra.
   - What historically dyed red lanterns, wedding silks and 春联 paper.
7. **Others, briefly:** 鸡血石 (cinnabar in dickite), 红珊瑚 (pigment?).

## Output
- report: one section per red, with a "physics", an "objects" and a "close-up look" subsection, numeric tables, sources and tags.
- At the end: a ranking of the reds by how iconic and how beautiful they are in a macro photograph, with one line of reasoning each.
- download.txt: one image URL per line, then a tab, then the object page, size and licence.
