# Studio lighting data for a physically based renderer

Use the supplied energy spectra for light colour and scale brightness separately. Reflectances below are **linear fractions**, not display RGB. **Measured** means a particular specimen was measured; **spec** means a manufacturer's nominal/reference value; **estimate** means an unverified typical value or an author's proposed setup. Museum descriptions are identified separately as reported practice, not radiometric measurements. Unpublished inputs remain unknown.

**Grey-card spectral data:** [Kodak's instruction sheet](https://docs.acescentral.com/assets/pdfs/kodak-grey-card.pdf#page=3), PDF p.3, “Spectral Reflectance,” plots grey/white sides over **400–700 nm** (**manufacturer reference graph**). No numerical table was recovered, so no exact spectral CSV is claimed. A flat **0.18** curve would be a **renderer approximation based on the nominal spec**, not measured spectral data. Independent historical results show why this distinction matters: [Palmer, *Field Standards of Reflectance*](https://www.asprs.org/wp-content/uploads/pers/1982journal/oct/1982_oct_1623-1625.pdf#page=2), printed p.1624, Tables 1–2, reports **measured** grey-card band reflectance factors **0.315 / 0.242 / 0.224** in actual passbands **418–489 / 507–580 / 597–690 nm**, respectively. These older-card, geometry-dependent broadband values are not monochromatic samples or a replacement for modern Kodak specifications.

The flock CSV contains only its explicit table points. Do not interpolate across the wide gaps and call the result a measured visible/NIR spectrum. A scalar albedo also cannot reproduce velvet's angular scattering.

The Aputure/Godox exports use the source's **energy** column, not photon counts. Original smoothing/thinning is retained; Aputure's artificial short-wave zero-fill is omitted. Small negative Aputure tail residuals are preserved as measurement/processing artifacts; they are not physical negative emission. The source label `0of9` has nonzero output. Independent peak normalization removes brightness differences between settings/heads. [Aputure processing script](https://github.com/aphalo/photobiologyLamps/blob/62fd04a618de16baccf2ab255ef019c87a1596cb/data-raw/Maya-video-LED/make-amaran-lamp-mspct.R) and Godox script above specify the processing. Neither a common CCT nor a high CRI establishes identical SPDs; these curves should remain model/configuration-specific.

For larger cine fixtures, a published numerical archive includes **ARRI LoCaster, L5-C and L7-C**: [Karge et al., project summary, Table 1](https://www.hdm-stuttgart.de/open-film-tools/en/publications/ProjectSummary.pdf) and [preserved data archive](https://zenodo.org/records/4642271). Its ZIP was not extractable through the available access methods; these ARRI curves are **not included**.

**Museum object-photography practice**

There is no single source-backed catalogue-lighting recipe across these institutions. A reported setup is evidence of use, not an independently measured geometry or an institution-wide standard. Dimensions and angles below are **reported practice**; recommendations are **author guidance/estimates**.

| Institution | Published photographic method; exact source location |
|---|---|
| **Met** | **Institutional guidance:** electronic flash/strobe for three-dimensional objects of every material. No numeric lamp positions, modifier sizes or backdrop prescription supplied. [*Care and Handling of Art Objects*](https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/The_Care_and_Handling_of_Art_Objects_Practices_in_The_Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art_2019.pdf#page=179), printed p.178, Barbara Bridgers, “Photographic Lighting.” |
| **Palace Museum** | **Reported texture/rollout setup:** **two flashes**, **60 × 60 cm** softboxes, front left/right at **45°**, grey paper over a glass turntable. Designed for consistent low-contrast images to stitch for publication/web use; not all catalogue photography. [Jin Yueping, *Capturing Surface Texture…*](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2020/11/13/u5fae29884d938.pdf#page=3), printed p.554, §一(一)2, Fig.2. **Alternative author guidance:** at least **four lamps**—top, key, fill, background—with object-dependent direction/power and diffusion or bounce. [Meng Chengguang, *博物馆之文物摄影*](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2020/11/13/u5fae2a6a1aea0.pdf#page=4), printed pp.581–582, “灯光的运用”/“背景的处理.” |

Description verified through the search index; direct fetch failed. |
| **Getty** | **Reported special rollout method:** clean, flat illumination to suppress shadows/reflections; staff explicitly contrast this with normal dimensional-object photography. No numerical lamp geometry, light type, modifier size or background given. [“Unrolling” a Porcelain Vase](https://www.getty.edu/news/vase-mounted-in-gilt-bronze-rollout-photography/), “Photographing the vase.” |
| **Smithsonian** | **Reported photogrammetry experiment**, not a general catalogue standard: absorbing backdrop/turntable cover simplifies masking but removes bounce fill beneath objects. [DPO, “Scaling up 3D Digitization”](https://dpo.si.edu/blog/scaling-3d-digitization-smithsonian), “Room for Improvement → Capture: Speed & Lighting” (index-readable; direct fetch failed). For texture rather than flat reproduction, [National Museums Scotland](https://www.nms.ac.uk/our-impact/national-work/training-and-guidance-for-museums/photographing-collections/lighting-and-colour), “Picking out fine detail and texture”/“Two lights,” recommends **one low raking light plus reflector**; its **two equal lights at 45°** recommendation is for flat paper/books (**guidance/estimate**, not a universal sculpture arrangement).

**FADGI/Metamorfoze:** [FADGI Technical Guidelines, third edition](https://www.digitizationguidelines.gov/guidelines/FADGITechnicalGuidelinesforDigitizingCulturalHeritageMaterials_ThirdEdition_05092023.pdf#page=6), contents p.6 and chapter 3 material tables, do not prescribe a sculpture studio layout. FADGI's separate [3D content category](https://www.digitizationguidelines.gov/guidelines/ccdo-subcat-3D1.html) addresses image sets/views. [Metamorfoze Image Quality v2.0](https://www.metamorfoze.nl/sites/default/files/documents/Preservation%20Imaging%20Guidelines%20English%202.0%2C%20April%202025.pdf#page=5), p.5, describes preservation of paper heritage. Neither is evidence for numerical catalogue-light placement for arbitrary 3D objects.

**Delivery limits:** the CSVs cover every numerical curve successfully retrieved, plus the explicitly sparse flock table. White/black seamless-paper values, a numerical Kodak spectrum, and an independently measured cloth-velvet scalar remain unresolved.
